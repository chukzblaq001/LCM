# LEARN CHEM 3.3 — Curriculum alignment

- JSS 1–3: Chemistry foundations are taught within the NERDC Basic Science and Technology curriculum context. NERDC publishes JSS 1–3 Basic Science and Technology as an integrated subject, not as a standalone JSS Chemistry subject.
- SS 1–3: Chemistry topics are organised around the NERDC revised Senior Secondary Education Curriculum and the commonly taught senior-secondary Chemistry sequence.
- University 100–400: These are broad undergraduate Chemistry study pathways (general, inorganic, organic, physical, analytical, materials, environmental and research chemistry). They are not claimed to be an NERDC university syllabus; course names and year placement vary by institution.
- JAMB: UTME preparation follows the topic areas and candidate abilities described in the official JAMB IBASS Chemistry syllabus.
- WAEC: Practice topics include theory, quantitative work, organic Chemistry, qualitative analysis and practical reasoning, supported by WAEC's Chemistry e-learning resource.
- NECO: Practice is aligned to senior-secondary Chemistry learning outcomes. Candidates should check the latest NECO syllabus and examination notices for their examination year.
